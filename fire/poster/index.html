Fire safety &
emergency response

Prevent fire. Know your extinguisher. Know your way out.
EVERY WORKER. EVERY SITE. EVERY DAY.
Use an extinguisher: P.A.S.S.
PPull

Pull the pin and break the seal.

AAim

Aim low, at the base of the fire.

SSqueeze

Squeeze the handle slowly.

SSweep

Sweep side to side until it is out.

Stop fire before it starts
  • No hot work without a permit and a fire watch
  • Switch off and cool the generator before refuelling
  • Store fuel and gas cylinders upright, away from offices
  • Smoke only in the marked smoking area
  • Keep fire points and exits clear at all times
  • Do not overload extension leads
In an emergency
997Fire
998Ambulance
999Police

Raise the alarm. Leave by the nearest exit. Go to the assembly point and wait for the head count. Never go back inside.
