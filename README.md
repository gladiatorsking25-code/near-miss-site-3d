# Near Miss Site 3D

Interactive 3D construction site for the Near Miss Awareness campaign
(Hydropower Energy & General Construction LLC, TAQA WS contract O-16124).

- Rotate and zoom the site, tap a number to see each near miss, and switch to
  **Show fixes** to see the controls in place.
- Press **Inspection walk** to follow the HSE Officer as he inspects the site,
  corrects each near miss and records it on ADOSH-SF Form G2 (sample entries
  for training).

Open it in any modern browser: the whole page is `index.html`.

Illustrative scenarios for training only.
